NotoSansKR-claims.ttf is a glyph subset of Noto Sans KR (Google, SIL Open Font License 1.1, see OFL.txt),
built from the @fontsource/noto-sans-kr package, covering only the closed vocabulary in ../vocab.py.
It is embedded in the synthetic claim documents and in nothing else.

NotoSansKR-forms.ttf is the same subset plus the printed labels of the two statutory forms redrawn in
../official_layouts.json (진단서, 진료비 계산서·영수증). It is embedded in the forms ../official_fill.py fills.
